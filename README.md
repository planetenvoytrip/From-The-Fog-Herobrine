## 📖 What Is From The Fog?

**From The Fog** is a psychological horror mod that reintroduces the classic Herobrine legend into modern Minecraft. Unlike modern horror mods that rely on constant chases and loud jumpscares, From The Fog focuses on **subtle dread** — the feeling of being watched, of things moving when you're not looking, of your world being subtly altered in ways you can't explain.

The entity is **immortal by design**. You cannot kill him. You cannot trap him. When you approach, he vanishes into the fog. The only way to "stop" him is to disable the mod or reduce his appearances through config.

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Psychological horror** — No constant chases, just dread
- ✅ **World manipulation** — He changes your world when you're not looking
- ✅ **Customizable intensity** — Adjust spawn rates, behaviors, and events
- ✅ **Shrine activation** — Build a Herobrine Shrine to provoke him immediately
- ✅ **In-game config** — Full control over every event
- ✅ **Resource pack included** — Custom skins, paintings, and items

## ✨ What He Does

### 👁️ Sightings
- Watches you from a distance, behind trees, or through windows
- Appears at the foot of your bed while you sleep
- Stands at the edge of your vision, then disappears when you turn

### 🏗️ World Manipulation
- Builds classic structures: sand pyramids, glowstone letters, and mossy cobblestone formations
- Leaves ominous signs with cryptic messages
- Strips leaves from entire groves of trees
- Mines tunnels near your location

### 🔥 Interaction
- Steals your torches and replaces them with redstone torches or soul lanterns
- Douses candles and changes your lighting
- Burns your base while you're away (configurable)
- Swaps paintings to haunted versions
- Possesses passive animals to watch you

### ⚔️ Combat & Jumpscares
- Sneaky strikes — punches you if you ignore him
- Jumpscare when you approach or attack (configurable)
- Can be configured to "crash" your game upon a jumpscare
- Prevents you from sleeping while he's nearby
